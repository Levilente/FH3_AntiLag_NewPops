# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release of **Anti-Lag for Forza Horizon 3**, on the [FH3 Mod Platform](https://github.com/Levilente/FH3_ModPlatform).

- **Garage > Upgrades > Engine > Turbo / Twin Turbo > Race Turbo w/ Anti-Lag** (the Race turbo plus 2 000 CR, `ExtraPrice` in the ini).
- Lifting the throttle above the cutoff RPM keeps the turbo's boost, so there is no lag on the way back.
- Exhaust pops and flames, played by the game itself; the game's own or the mod's backfire samples (**Mods > Anti-Lag > Backfire sounds**).
- **Tuning > Engine** (the platform's ENGINE tab): pressure decay, boost retention, cutoff RPM and pop interval, per car.
- Log off by default: `AntiLag.ini` `[AntiLag]` `Log=1` writes the mod's ordinary lines to `platform.log`; problems are always logged.
- Needs FH3 Mod Platform **1.4.2** or newer. Offline only.
